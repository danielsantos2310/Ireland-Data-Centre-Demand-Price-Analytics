# Design system

## Creative direction
Evidence-first energy journalism: dark navy analytical canvas, cyan for demand, amber for price/cost pressure, pale mint for defensible findings, muted grey for limitations.

## Palette
- Background: #07131C
- Surface: #0D1E29
- Surface 2: #102936
- Text: #F4F8FA
- Muted: #9AB0BA
- Cyan: #69D8E7
- Amber: #F3B45D
- Mint: #7ED8A4
- Coral: #F27668
- Rule: #233945

## Typography
System sans-serif. Monospace labels for section numbers, evidence tags and source labels.

## Interaction
No external charting dependency. Canvas charts redraw responsively. Narrative remains readable with JavaScript disabled.
